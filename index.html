Winter practice in Utah County is broken.

From November to March, snow, cold, and early dark shut down outdoor ranges. Right now your options are:

30–45 min

Drive to Salt Lake County

The nearest heated ranges are in South Jordan and South Salt Lake. On a school night, nobody's making that drive.

$49/hr

Pay for a simulator

Simulators are useful, but it's not the same as watching a real ball fly.

Party mode

Go to Topgolf

Fun with friends, but it's built around games, food, and hourly bay rentals, not practice.

Or you stop until spring and lose everything you gained over the summer. Stripeshow fixes that.

What you get

  • 20

    Heated, covered bays

  • 0
